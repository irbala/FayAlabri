Athkar

A calm Arabic web app for the morning and evening adhkar, designed for phones first. It shows one dhikr at a time, with a tap-to-count button that keeps track of the repetitions and moves on when each one is complete.

WebCraft

A beginner-friendly coding playground. Each short lesson explains one idea in HTML, CSS or JavaScript, with a code editor, a live preview and a checklist. Finishing lessons earns coins that unlock new worlds, and progress is saved in the browser.

Dr. Khalid Alabri

A website for a certified trainer and coach. Visitors can read about him, browse photos from his talks and events, send a message through a contact form, and follow a link to book sessions through his online store. Hand-written HTML and CSS in Arabic, right to left.

Ateeq Store

A concept store for a Saudi perfume brand, in Arabic. Customers can browse the perfumes, add them to a cart, change quantities and see the total. The cart is saved in the browser as they move between pages.

Deema's Art Portfolio

A creative showcase portfolio for Sirendema, highlighting her artistic talent and versatility. This project emphasizes visual storytelling and provides an immersive experience for art enthusiasts.

Twitch Chat Viewer

A customizable viewer for any streamer's Twitch chat, with light, dark and cozy themes and a custom background color. Preferences are saved in the browser.

London Trip Planner

An Arabic travel planner for London and Brighton with cafes, restaurants, shopping and things to do. Every place links to Instagram and Google Maps, and the home page shows the weather.

Edinburgh Travel Guide

A bilingual guide to Edinburgh's cafes, restaurants, shopping and nature spots, with a fixed menu to jump between sections and a map link for every place.

Bodrum Travel Guide

An Arabic one-page guide to Bodrum's restaurants, shopping and places to visit, with a full-width hero and photo cards that open each place's Instagram page.

Naqti Design concept

A design concept for a camel marketplace, with search, filters and listing cards in a warm desert palette. It is a static mock-up, so the controls are for show.
